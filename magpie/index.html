Magpie

Collect the things you'd love. Your people see what to get you. The surprises stay secret.

For families

A child collects wishes from shops or by snapping a photo. Their grown-up decides what the family sees. Givers reserve presents, and the child never finds out who's getting what.

For grown-ups

Share your own lists with one link. Anyone with it can reserve something, and you never see what's been reserved.

Private by design

Everything lives in your own iCloud. There is no Magpie server, no ads and no tracking.
