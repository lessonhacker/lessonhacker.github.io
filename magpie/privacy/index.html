Magpie privacy policy

Last updated 24 October 2026

Magpie is a wishlist app for a family: a child collects things she'd love, her grown-up decides what the family sees, and the people who buy presents reserve them. It's built for one family's own use and shared with a few relatives through Apple's TestFlight.

What Magpie stores, and where

Everything Magpie saves lives on your devices and in the grown-up's own iCloud account, using Apple's CloudKit. That's the lists, wishes, photos, the shops chosen for the child's browser, and gift reservations. There is no Magpie server. Nobody who makes Magpie can read any of it.

The grown-up shares the lists with people they invite, using iCloud sharing. The child is never invited to the part of iCloud that holds reservations, so she can't see what has been bought, and givers are never invited to the part that holds wishes the grown-up has hidden.

Children

A child uses Magpie with her own Apple Child Account, which her grown-up manages through Family Sharing. Magpie never asks a child for an email address, phone number or date of birth, and has no sign-in of its own. A grown-up sets the family up and invites her.

No tracking, no ads, no analytics

Magpie contains no advertising, no analytics and no tracking of any kind, and doesn't share data with anyone.

When Magpie talks to the internet

  • Shop pages. When a wish is added from a shop's website, Magpie reads that page (its title, picture and price) and downloads the product photo, the same as a web browser would.
  • Website safety check. For a website that isn't one of the approved shops, Magpie looks up when the website's address was registered, using the public RDAP registry at rdap.org. Only the website's address is sent.
  • Nearby shops. When the child snaps a photo, Magpie asks Apple Maps which shops are nearby so she can pick the one she's in. Only the shop's name is saved, never where she was.
  • Invite links. A Magpie link opens a page at lessonhacker.github.io if Magpie isn't installed. Whose lists it is and the iCloud invitation sit in the part of the link after the #, which browsers never send to a website, so the page can't see or record them.
  • Finding a page. When the grown-up uses Find it, Magpie opens a web search for the item.

Apple Intelligence

On devices that have it, Magpie uses Apple's on-device model to read the text on a photo and to shorten long shop names. This runs on the device.

Camera, photos and location

Magpie asks before using the camera, the photo library or location, and uses them only for the things above. Any of them can be turned off in Settings.

Deleting data

Deleting a wish or list removes it from iCloud for everyone in the family. The grown-up can stop sharing with anyone from the People screen. Removing Magpie from a device and choosing to remove its iCloud data in Settings deletes everything it stored.
