Look after their lists

They'd like you to look after their Magpie wishlists, so the family can see them and the surprises stay secret.

Here's how

  1. Get Magpie on your iPhone or iPad.
  2. Tap Set up lists for my child and type their name.
  3. Let them scan the code on your screen with their iPhone or iPad.

Everything they've added already moves across to the new lists.
